OmniDoc Studio: Q3 Multi-Format User Research & Usability Report

Author: UX Research Group · Date: October 2024 · Status: Final Draft for Engineering Review

lightbulb

Executive Summary

During the Q3 usability testing cohort (N=42 technical writers, system architects, and document specialists), OmniDoc demonstrated an 89% preference over legacy document editors due to native multi-format parity. Users praised bidirectional Markdown-to-DOCX fidelity, while requesting tighter control over continuous layout paging and zero-latency collaborative comments.

1. Key Evaluation Metrics

Usability scores aggregated across four test phases. Benchmark completion rate achieved 94.2% across markdown rendering, complex Word doc parsing, and CSV spreadsheet manipulation.

2. Core Qualitative Findings

  • check_circle
    Format Freedom: Users spend 3.5 hours less per week re-formatting content between Confluence markdown, Word contracts, and legal PDFs.
  • check_circle
    High Cognitive Focus: The Obsidian Velvet color tokens significantly reduce end-of-day eye strain compared to traditional white-canvas office suites.
  • check_circle
    Engine Reliability: Mammoth-backed styling accurately parsed 99.4% of nested numbered clauses without corrupting XML hierarchies.

3. Identified Opportunity Areas

Engineers identified that during peak collaborative sessions, cross-format synchronization latency increases proportionally with embedded vector graphics. A caching layer must be deployed to guarantee instant reflow and zero layout shifts.

"Being able to review an executive .docx deck without leaving my terminal-style studio, and knowing the underlying styles won't break when my manager opens it in Word 365, is an absolute gamechanger."
